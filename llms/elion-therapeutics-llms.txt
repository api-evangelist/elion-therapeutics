## About

Elion Therapeutics is a privately held, clinical-stage biotechnology company working on
treatments for invasive fungal infections (IFIs). Its lead candidate, SF001, is a
rationally designed analog of amphotericin B intended to retain broad fungicidal
activity while mitigating the systemic toxicity that limits the parent molecule. SF001
received FDA Qualified Infectious Disease Product (QIDP) and Fast Track designations in
2023 and has moved from a first-in-human single-ascending-dose study into
multiple-ascending-dose evaluation. The company closed an $81 million Series B in June
2024 led by Deerfield Management and the AMR Action Fund, with participation from
Illinois Ventures.
